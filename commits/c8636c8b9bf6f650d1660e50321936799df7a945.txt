message:
orchestrator: read blocked-by edges written with markdown emphasis
Ticket stores commonly write edges as `**Blocked by:** 67`. The frontmatter
parser required the line to start with 'blocked by' after whitespace only, so
every emphasised edge was ignored: the dependency map came out empty, every
ready-stated ticket looked startable, and a blocked ticket could be started.
Emphasis is now stripped before the match.
